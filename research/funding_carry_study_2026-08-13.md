## Honest caveats

- Corpus is 7 recording days (07-19, 07-21, 08-08..08-13) with partial
  days; 232 hourly points ~ 69% of a
  full 7-day x 2-symbol x 24h grid. Regime fractions are indicative,
  not statistically settled.
- Hyperliquid funds hourly; the study uses the raw hourly rate (no
  8h normalization). OI weighting is trivial here (single venue).
- `mp-query carry` weights by OI contracts (Hyperliquid reports no
  notional); with a second venue reporting USD notional the two are
  never mixed (unit-class rule in `analytics::carry_series`).
- Basis is mark-vs-oracle (the venue's own index); it is NOT a
  tradable spot basis -- that needs a spot leg (BACKLOG: spot venues).
